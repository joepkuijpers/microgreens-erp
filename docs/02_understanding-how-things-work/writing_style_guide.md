# Writing Style Guide

This document defines the writing principles used throughout the Preservation Energy Architecture book.

Its purpose is not to define the content.

Its purpose is to define how the content should be written.

Every chapter should feel like a journey of discovery rather than a collection of explanations.

---

# 1. Teach Through Discovery

Do not simply explain.

Lead the reader towards discovering the principle themselves.

A good section answers one question while naturally creating curiosity for the next.

The reader should regularly think:

> "I had never considered it that way."

---

# 2. Every Section Must Introduce One New Idea

Avoid repeating the same principle using different examples.

Every section should answer a different question.

The book should continuously move forward.

If two sections teach the same lesson, merge or remove one.

---

# 3. Vary the Rhythm

Not every section should follow the same structure.

Alternate between different writing styles.

Examples include:

* Discovery
* Story
* Contrast
* Reflection
* Challenge
* Principle

Changing the rhythm keeps the reader engaged.

---

# 4. Explain Why Before How

Readers remember reasons far longer than procedures.

Always establish why something matters before explaining how it works.

---

# 5. One Memorable Sentence

Every section should contain one sentence the reader remembers.

Examples:

> Every harvest starts many clocks.

> Biological variation is not a flaw.

> Technology should adapt to biology, not biology to technology.

The memorable sentence should summarise the central idea without becoming a slogan.

---

# 6. Vary Sentence Length

Alternate between short and longer sentences.

Example:

Biology changes.

Sometimes slowly.

Sometimes so gradually that the change goes unnoticed until the difference has become impossible to ignore.

Rhythm improves readability.

---

# 7. Respect the Reader

Do not over-explain.

Assume the reader is intelligent.

Trust them to connect ideas.

Explain only what is necessary.

---

# 8. Build Like a Staircase

Each section should build upon the previous one.

Never restart the explanation.

Never return to concepts that have already been established unless they are viewed from a completely different perspective.

---

# 9. Curiosity Before Information

Facts are important.

Curiosity is what keeps people reading.

Whenever possible, begin with a question, observation, contradiction or surprising statement.

---

# 10. Finish with Reflection

Avoid ending sections with a summary.

Instead, leave the reader with an idea worth thinking about.

The end of one section should naturally lead towards the beginning of the next.

---

# 11. Simplicity Creates Depth

Professional writing is not complicated writing.

Use precise language.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Complex ideas should become easier to understand, not more difficult.

---

# 12. Focus Determines Progress

Every chapter should remain focused on answering one central question.

Interesting side questions will always appear.

Record them.

Do not allow them to interrupt the journey.

A good book is not one that answers every question.

It is one that answers the right questions in the right order.
