# Water Structure and Molecular Change

## Purpose

This Engineering Extension connects the book's explanations of water, molecular movement, temperature, drying, cooling, freezing, thawing, and freeze-drying.

The purpose is to understand why water is not simply a quantity that can be removed, added, frozen, or evaporated. Water participates in the structure and behaviour of biological material.

The central engineering question is:

> How does the physical state and availability of water influence biological material during processing and preservation?

## Engineering Questions That Follow

- Where is water located within biological material?
- How easily can different forms of water move?
- How does temperature affect molecular movement?
- What happens to structure when water is removed?
- What happens when water is frozen?
- What changes when frozen material thaws?
- How do pressure and temperature interact during freeze-drying?
- How can water removal be measured?
- How can the preservation result be validated?

These questions lead into freeze-dryer design, energy analysis, measurement strategy, process control, packaging, and product validation.
